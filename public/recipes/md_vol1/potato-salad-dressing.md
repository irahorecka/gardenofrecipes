# Potato Salad Dressing

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp flour
- 3/4 cup vinegar (I start with less and add more if needed to taste right)

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs
2. Add the sugar, milk, mustard, salt, and flour
3. Add the vinegar
4. Cook until the mixture starts to thicken
5. Pour over diced potatoes
