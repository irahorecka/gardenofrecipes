# 24 Hour Fruit Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 7-oz jar pitted sweet cherries, cut and drained
- 2 small cans pineapple tidbits, drained
- 3 oranges, peeled and cut up
- 1 cup miniature marshmallows
- 2 beaten eggs
- 2 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice
- 2 tbsp pineapple juice
- 1 tbsp oleo
- a dash of salt
- 3/4 cup chilled whipping cream

## Instructions

1. Combine the cherries, pineapple, oranges, and marshmallows. Use the dressing below on this and chill 24 hours
2. Combine the beaten eggs, vinegar or lemon juice, pineapple juice, oleo, and salt
3. Put in a sauce pan and heat it, stirring constantly until it thickens. Cool
4. Whip the chilled whipping cream and add it into the cooled egg mixture
5. Put over fruit and chill
