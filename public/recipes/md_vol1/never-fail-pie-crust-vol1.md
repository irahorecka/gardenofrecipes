# Never Fail Pie Crust

Very tender

Makes 3 pie crusts

## Ingredients

- 3 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp vinegar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/3 cups shortening
- 5 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Blend the dry ingredients with the shortening
2. Add the beaten egg and liquid
3. Form into a ball
4. Wrap in waxed paper in the ice box
5. Take off as needed and warm to room temperature
6. Roll on floured board
7. Use as wanted
