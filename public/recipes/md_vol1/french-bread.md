# French Bread

Good

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp dry yeast
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 tsp sugar (for the yeast)
- 3/4 cup hot water
- 2 tbsp butter or oleo
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- about 3 1/2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water with 1 tsp sugar added
2. Mix together the hot water, butter or oleo, sugar, and salt. Let it all dissolve and cool gradually
3. Add the dissolved yeast mixture and about 3 1/2 cups flour
4. Knead until it does not stick to your hands
5. Cover and let rise for about 10 minutes
6. Knead by hand again. Let it rise
7. Roll this out to 8 x 14 inches, then roll as a jelly roll
8. Place on a greased cookie sheet. Shape the ends so that they are pointed
9. Make diagonal slashes across the top with a sharp knife — about 5 slashes, each about 1/4 inch into the dough
10. Brush the top with melted oleo. Let rise until doubled
11. Bake at 400 degrees for 10 minutes, then reduce to 350 degrees and bake for an additional 25 minutes or until done
