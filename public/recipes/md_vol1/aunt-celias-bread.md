# Aunt Celia's Bread

## Ingredients

- 2 cups warm water
- 1 yeast cake or 1 tbsp dry yeast
- 1 1/2 tbsp salad oil
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 tbsp sugar
- flour, enough for a soft dough (but stiff enough for bread)

## Instructions

1. Mix the warm water and yeast together and let the yeast dissolve
2. Add the salad oil, salt, sugar, and enough flour for a soft dough that is stiff enough for bread
3. Let it rise
4. Make into 2 loaves
5. Let rise until doubled
6. Bake at 375 degrees for 30 minutes
