# Extra Good Apple or Pear Cake

Very moist

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs, beaten well
- 1 3/4 cups sugar
- 1 cup oil
- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 cups peeled and chopped apples or pears
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs well
2. Add the sugar and oil
3. Add the dry ingredients
4. Last, add the apples and pecans
5. Bake at 350 degrees in a 9x13 pan, greased and flour-dusted
6. You can also bake in a Bundt pan for 40 to 50 minutes. Check with a toothpick before 40 minutes

## Burnt Sugar Frosting

### Ingredients

- 1 stick oleo
- 1 1/2 tsp white Karo
- 2 cups sugar
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tbsp vanilla
- 1 cup pecans

### Instructions

1. Mix together and bring to a boil
2. Boil 1 minute, stirring constantly
3. Beat well with the electric mixer
4. Cool and add the pecans
5. Use on cake
