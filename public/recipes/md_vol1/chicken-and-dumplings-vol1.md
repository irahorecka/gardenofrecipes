# Chicken & Dumplings

From Aunt Celia, Old Rosenberg Book

## Chicken

1. Cut up a fryer. Wash and drain it
2. Season with salt and pepper and roll in flour
3. Fry to brown lightly on each side
4. Add a chopped onion
5. Add water and cook slowly until tender
6. Cool the chicken and remove the bones (or you may leave the pieces without taking the meat off the bone)
7. Thicken the broth for gravy. This will require a good deal of broth as the dumplings will simmer in it. To thicken the gravy, add 3 tbsp flour to cold water, mix well, and add to the broth while stirring constantly
8. Cook a while. Be sure it is seasoned good to suit taste
