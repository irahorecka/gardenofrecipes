# Rolled Sugar Cookies

Yoakum Beauty Shop Book

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter or oleo
- 2 eggs
- 3 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 2 tsp vanilla or 1 tsp lemon extract
- 2 tsp baking powder

## Instructions

1. Cream the oleo and sugar
2. Add the beaten eggs and flavor
3. Add gradually the sifted dry ingredients
4. Mix the dough and chill it a while. It rolls better
5. Roll on floured board
6. Cut and bake at 350 degrees for 6 to 8 minutes

Very good!!!

Note: these are very soft, and they must be well-chilled or they will be too soft to cut. When you do cut these, flour the board well. Take out and roll only a little of the dough and leave the rest in the refrigerator until ready to roll the next batch. I lift them using a spatula.
