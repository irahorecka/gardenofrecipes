# Biscuits

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 3 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 tbsp Crisco
- 3/4 cup to 1 cup milk

## Instructions

1. Sift flour first, and measure by dipping into the sifted flour with a spoon
2. Sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt
3. Add the Crisco (do not melt it). Use a blending fork until it's all crumbled
4. Add about 3/4 cup to 1 cup milk to make a soft dough
5. Roll on a floured board and cut with a biscuit cutter
6. You may grease the pan and then touch the biscuit to the greased pan
7. Put the biscuit in the pan, and let the greased part of the biscuit be on top
8. Bake in a preheated 450 degree oven for 12 to 15 minutes or until light tan
9. For drop biscuits, use more milk to make a softer dough and drop it in the pan from a spoon
