# Dressing for Leaf Lettuce

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp or less sugar
- 1/4 tsp dry mustard
- a dash of salt and pepper
- 1 beaten egg
- 3 tbsp vinegar
- 1 tbsp oleo or butter
- milk or cream, to thin
- finely sliced onion greens, if wanted

## Instructions

1. Mix together the sugar, dry mustard, salt, and pepper
2. Add the beaten egg and vinegar
3. Cook until thick
4. Add the oleo or butter and cool
5. Thin with milk or cream
6. Pour over lettuce. Finely sliced onion greens may be added
