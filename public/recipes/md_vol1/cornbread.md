# Cornbread

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 4 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 3-4 tbsp sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 3 tbsp oleo, melted

## Instructions

1. Sift together flour, cornmeal, baking powder, and salt
2. Add the beaten egg (2 eggs make it more tender), milk, and melted oleo
3. A thin batter makes better cornbread. Do not overbeat
4. Pour into a round pan or a 9 x 9 x 2 inch pan, greased and flour-dusted
5. Bake in a pre-heated oven at 450 degrees for about 20 minutes. This depends upon your own oven
