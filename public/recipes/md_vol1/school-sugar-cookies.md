# School Sugar Cookies

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 5 to 6 cups flour
- 1 cup Crisco or oleo
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp baking soda

## Instructions

1. Cream the sugar and Crisco
2. Add the eggs
3. Sift the dry ingredients gradually with the milk until all the flour is used
4. You may use lemon extract or lemon rind instead of vanilla
5. Mix the dough and you may chill the dough if you want
6. Roll on floured board
7. Cut and put in greased pan
8. Bake at 350 degrees

These are good.
