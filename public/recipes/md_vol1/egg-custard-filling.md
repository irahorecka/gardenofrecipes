# Egg Custard Filling

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- nutmeg, for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs very well
2. Add the sugar, milk, and vanilla
3. Mix well
4. Pour into the unbaked pie shell
5. Sprinkle with nutmeg and bake as stated above

Note: may mix this custard with 1 cup of cooked rice and bake this mixture. Use sugar as wanted. If needed, add a little more of it.
