# Oatmeal Cookies from School

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter or Crisco
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp soda
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 cups oatmeal

## Instructions

1. Cream together the butter or Crisco, white sugar, and vanilla
2. Add the flour, soda, pecans, salt, and oatmeal to the creamed mixture
3. May use as drop cookies, roll into balls, or wrap in waxed paper, chill, and slice
4. Bake at 350 degrees
5. May add spices if wanted
