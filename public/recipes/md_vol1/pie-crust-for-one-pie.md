# Pie Crust for One Pie

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 6 tbsp soft oleo
- 1 beaten egg

## Instructions

1. Sift the flour and sugar
2. Cut in the soft oleo
3. Stir in the beaten egg and make into a ball
4. Roll on floured board 1/8 inch thick
5. Put in a pie pan and flute edges
6. Pour the custard into the shell and bake 10 minutes at 450 degrees
7. Then bake at 325 degrees for 30 minutes or till a knife put into the center comes out clean
