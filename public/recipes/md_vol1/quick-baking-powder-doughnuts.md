# Quick Baking Powder Doughnuts

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp shortening or oleo
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 cup plus 2 tbsp milk

## Instructions

1. Mix all together and let rest for 10 minutes (take a nap)
2. Roll dough on a floured board until it is 1/4 inch thick
3. Cut into squares and make small gashes in the dough with a knife
4. Fry and sprinkle with sugar
