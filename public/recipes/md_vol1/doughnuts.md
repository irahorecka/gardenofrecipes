# Doughnuts

These are good

## Ingredients

- 1 yeast or 1 pkg yeast
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1/2 cup milk, scalded
- 1/3 cup oleo
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup flour
- 2 beaten eggs
- 2 to 2 1/2 cups more flour (about 3 3/4 cups flour in all)

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water
2. Scald the milk and add the oleo, sugar, and salt
3. Cool this and add the yeast mixture
4. Stir in 1 cup flour, the beaten eggs, and 2 to 2 1/2 cups more flour. Add just enough flour to make a soft dough
5. Cover and let rise
6. Make into doughnuts — roll part of the dough on a floured board and cut with a doughnut cutter
7. Let rise, then fry. Glaze
8. This dough may also be used for kolaches, rolls, or coffee cake
