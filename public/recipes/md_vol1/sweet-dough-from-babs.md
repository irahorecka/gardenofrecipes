# Sweet Dough from Babs

I won first place with this

## Ingredients

- 1 cup milk, scalded
- 1/4 cup Crisco
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 pkg yeast or 1 tbsp dry yeast or 1 cake yeast
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 tsp sugar (for the yeast)
- 1 beaten egg
- about 3 1/2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. To the scalded milk, add the Crisco, sugar, and salt. Let cool
2. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water. Add 1 tsp sugar
3. Mix all together and add the beaten egg and about 3 1/2 cups flour to make a soft dough
4. Butter the top of the dough and mix by hand to butter all of the dough
5. Cover with Saran wrap or foil and let it rise in a warm place
6. Then make into desired shapes or rolls. Can use as coffee cakes, kolaches, or doughnuts
7. Keep the dough soft. Too much flour makes it tough
