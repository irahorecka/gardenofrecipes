# Pepper Cabbage

## Ingredients

- 3 cups shredded cabbage
- 1/4 cup chopped carrots
- 1/2 cup finely chopped sweet red pepper
- 1/4 cup chopped celery
- 2 tbsp sugar, to suit taste
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 cup cider vinegar, to suit taste

## Instructions

1. Shred the cabbage
2. Add the carrots, red pepper, and celery
3. Add the sugar, salt, and black pepper. Mix well
4. Pour the cider vinegar over this

This keeps in the ice box covered for 1 week.
