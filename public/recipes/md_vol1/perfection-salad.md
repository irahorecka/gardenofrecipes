# Perfection Salad

## Ingredients

- 1 package lime Jello
- 2 cups boiling water
- 2 tbsp vinegar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup shredded cabbage
- 1 cup diced celery
- 1 green pepper
- pimentos, to taste

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the lime Jello in the boiling water
2. Add the vinegar and salt. Cool
3. Add the cabbage, celery, green pepper, and pimentos
4. Chill until firm
