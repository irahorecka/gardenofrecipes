# Ice Box Cookies from Granny Schott

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 3 small eggs
- 1 tsp cream of tartar
- 3 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp soda
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. Cream the butter, sugar, and eggs
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and roll into a sausage form
3. Wrap in waxed paper and chill
4. Slice and bake
