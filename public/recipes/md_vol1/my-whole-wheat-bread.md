# My Whole Wheat Bread

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups very warm water
- 1 pkg yeast
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp melted shortening
- 1 cup whole wheat flour
- 3 cups white flour
- 1 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, dissolve the yeast and sugar in the warm water
2. Mix in the rest of the ingredients
3. Knead until it doesn't stick to your hands
4. Grease the top of the dough and turn it so that all sides are greased
5. Cover with plastic wrap and let rise for 15 minutes
6. Punch the dough down and knead in the bowl a few times, then cover again
7. Let the dough rise until doubled (a finger stuck in the dough should leave an impression that remains)
8. Put the dough on a floured board. Divide into 2 balls
9. Cover with the mixing bowl and let set for 10 minutes
10. Shape into loaves and put into greased loaf pans. Grease the top of each loaf
11. Let rise until double
12. Bake at 375 degrees for 30 to 35 minutes or until the loaf sounds hollow when tapped
13. Take the bread from the oven and butter the top
14. Put the bread on a cake rack, then inside a large paper bag. Close the bag and leave the bread there until it is cooled
15. To make plain white bread, substitute 1 cup of white flour for the whole wheat flour
