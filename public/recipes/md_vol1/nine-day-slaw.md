# Nine Day Slaw

## Ingredients

- 1 medium head shredded cabbage
- 2 sticks diced celery
- 2 medium onions, diced
- 1 green pepper, diced
- pimentos, if you choose
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 cup salad oil, or less (you may use only 3/4 cup)
- 3/4 cup vinegar
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp sugar

## Instructions

1. Combine the cabbage, celery, onions, green pepper, and pimentos
2. Mix together the sugar, salad oil, vinegar, salt, and the additional sugar
3. Cook the dressing just to boiling and pour over the vegetables
4. Mix and let cool
5. Cover and store in the ice box at least 1 day before serving

This stays fresh for 9 days. When I served this the first time, all asked why I didn't make it before. I said, "This is the first time I made it." It was in the San Antonio Light.
