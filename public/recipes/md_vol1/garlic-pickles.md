# Garlic Pickles

Barbara's favorites

## Ingredients

- 2 serrano peppers, per quart jar
- 2 pods of garlic, per quart jar
- fresh dill, per quart jar
- grape leaves, per quart jar
- onions, may also be added
- nice cukes, to fill the quarts
- 8 cups water (rain water is best)
- 1/2 cup canning salt
- 4 cups 90% vinegar

## Instructions

1. In each quart jar, put the serrano peppers, garlic, fresh dill, and grape leaves. Onions may also be added
2. Fill the quarts with nice cukes
3. Boil together the water, canning salt, and vinegar
4. Pour this over the cucumbers, leaving 1 inch head space
5. Seal tight
6. Wait for 6 weeks and eat

I found this years back in a San Antonio paper. It was from a Mrs. C. Wells.
