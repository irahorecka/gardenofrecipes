# Ginger Cookies

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup shortening
- 2 tsp soda
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp ginger
- 4 tbsp homemade or grandma's molasses
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp cloves
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 1/4 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Cream the shortening and sugar
2. Add the beaten egg and molasses
3. Sift the dry ingredients and mix (you may need to add 2 more tbsp of flour)
4. Form into small balls
5. Dip one side in sugar
6. Put on greased pan about 1 inch apart
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 12 minutes

These have a cracked sugar glaze on top. They are very good. Makes 5 dozen. The dough may be chilled first, then make into balls.
