# Cranberry Sauce

Delicious. Must for Turkey Day and Christmas

## Ingredients

- 1 pound bag fresh or frozen cranberries
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 to 2 cups water
- 2 packages raspberry Jello
- 2 cups boiling water
- 1 cup chopped pecans
- 1 cup crushed pineapple

## Instructions

1. Combine the cranberries, sugar, and water in a sauce pan
2. Heat to boiling, stirring to dissolve all the sugar
3. Boil rapidly until the berries pop open (5 to 10 minutes)
4. Dissolve the raspberry Jello in the boiling water. Add the pecans and crushed pineapple (if you use the pineapple juice, measure the juice and deduct that amount from the amount of water used for dissolving the Jello — if you have 1/2 cup of pineapple juice, then use only 1 1/2 cups of water when dissolving the Jello; this is important or else it will not jell right)
5. Add the Jello, pineapple, and nuts to the cranberries
6. Put it into a large Pyrex casserole
7. Let it cool
8. Cover and put it in the ice box

Delicious.
