# Peanut Butter Cookies

These came from Grit Magazine, and I made these in school

## Ingredients

- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup shortening
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp soda

## Instructions

1. Cream together the peanut butter, brown sugar, eggs, shortening, white sugar, and vanilla
2. Sift together the flour, salt, and soda
3. Mix this together and roll into small balls
4. Put on ungreased cookie sheet pans about 2 inches apart
5. Criss cross with a fork or the bottom part of a hammer that one uses to tenderize steak
6. Dip either the fork or bottom of hammer in sugar first, then put it on the cookie. This works nice and leaves a nice design
7. Bake at 350 degrees for 7 to 12 minutes
