# Apple Strudel Dough

## Ingredients

- 3/4 stick butter or oleo, melted
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 egg yolk only
- about 1 3/4 to 2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter or oleo. Add the milk and egg yolk
2. Gradually add the flour to make a very soft dough
3. Mix and divide into 3 or 4 balls
4. Put each ball in a sandwich bag and put in the icebox to chill about 3 hours
5. Take out 1 ball at a time and roll on a floured board or table (a table is preferred because it's larger)
6. Keep rolling and turning the dough over, adding more flour each time, until the dough is really thin
7. Put a clean cup towel on the table and dust it with a little flour
8. Put the dough on the towel and add the filling below

## Apple Strudel Filling

### Ingredients

- butter or oleo, melted, for brushing
- about 1 cup coconut (if wanted)
- 1 cup chopped pecans
- about 6 to 8 sliced apples (tart apples preferred over Delicious apples)
- about 1 1/2 to 2 cups crushed vanilla wafer crumbs
- 3/4 cup raisins
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon

### Instructions

1. Brush melted butter or oleo across the dough until it is about 1 inch from the edges. Do not butter too much
2. Mix together the coconut, pecans, apples, vanilla wafer crumbs, and raisins. Put this in a large bowl, then divide it into the same number of portions as strudels you plan to make
3. Also mix together the sugar and cinnamon. Divide this between the number of strudels you make

## How to Make the Strudel

1. On the rolled out strudel dough (on the cup towel), sprinkle the portion of filling that goes with this strudel
2. Then sprinkle on the portion of sugar mixture that goes with this strudel
3. Roll the dough as you would roll a jelly roll, using the cup towel to guide the dough
4. Have a piece of aluminum foil ready — large enough to cover the strudel completely, if you choose to do so
5. Gently guide the dough off of the cup towel and onto the foil
6. Place the foil on a baking sheet and fold the sides of the foil over themselves. Do not cover the strudel top with the foil — what you make looks like a little bread pan, but made around the strudel. Bake the strudel uncovered; when it comes from the oven you can use the same foil to cover the strudel
7. Brush the top of the strudel dough with just a little butter or oleo. You can top it with a little more cinnamon and sugar if you choose
8. Do the same with the other strudels
9. Bake at 350 degrees until golden brown, about 30 to 40 minutes
10. Brush them with butter or oleo again when they come from the oven. Let them cool before eating
11. If you freeze these, use the foil the strudel was baked on and seal it around the strudel tightly
