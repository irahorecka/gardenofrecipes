# Old Fashioned Wilted Lettuce

Very good!

## Ingredients

- 4 slices bacon, diced
- 1/2 cup vinegar
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp pepper
- 1 head shredded lettuce
- chopped onion, if you choose

## Instructions

1. Fry the diced bacon until it is crisp
2. Add the vinegar, water, sugar, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil
3. Pour over the shredded lettuce (you may add chopped onion if you choose, but I don't)
4. Toss and serve immediately
