# Oatmeal Crunchies from Lillie

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sifted flour
- 1/2 tsp soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup shortening
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 cup pecans
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 3/4 cup oatmeal
- sugar, for dipping

## Instructions

1. Sift all dry ingredients
2. Add brown sugar, shortening, egg, vanilla, and beat well
3. Stir in oatmeal and pecans
4. Form dough into small balls
5. Dip in a little sugar and put on ungreased pans
6. Bake at 375 degrees for 10 to 12 minutes

This makes about 3 1/2 dozen cookies. I always double the recipe.
