# 110 Year Old Pie Crust

From Hallettsville Tribune Herald, recipe from a Pam Wolf

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups flour
- 1 cup shortening
- 1 tsp vinegar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 egg

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour and salt
2. Cut in the shortening and set aside
3. Mix the egg and vinegar, and add enough water to make 2/3 cup liquid
4. Add to the flour mixture, using as much as needed
