# Quick Buttermilk Rolls

From Co-op Cook Book

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp dry yeast
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 tbsp sugar (for the yeast)
- 1 cup warm buttermilk
- 1/4 tsp soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 3 to 4 tbsp soft butter or oleo
- 2 1/2 to 3 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water. Add 1 tbsp sugar
2. When dissolved, add all of these ingredients except the flour to the warm buttermilk
3. Then add the yeast mixture
4. Add enough of the flour to make a soft dough
5. Knead on a board until it has an elastic feel
6. Cover and let rise 10 to 15 minutes or more, until it has doubled
7. Roll and cut out in any shape
8. Put in a greased pan. Let rise again until doubled
9. Bake in a 375 degree oven, preheated to 400 degrees
10. Brush with butter or oleo and serve warm
