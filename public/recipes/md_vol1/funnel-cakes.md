# Funnel Cakes

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 cups milk
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 4 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- fat, for frying
- powdered sugar (optional)

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs. Add the milk and sugar and mix
2. Sift the flour, salt, and baking powder together
3. Add the dry ingredients to the egg and milk mixture. Beat the batter until smooth. It should be thin enough to run through a small funnel with a spout opening at the bottom of 1/4 inch
4. Heat fat for frying at 375 degrees
5. Hold a finger over the bottom of the funnel and ladle batter into the funnel — about 1/4 cup — or fill the funnel and release your finger when you have one swirl made
6. Start in the center and swirl around and around until the cake is 3 or 4 inches in diameter. For crisp cakes, keep the spirals thin. For fatter, chewier ones, let the cakes get thicker
7. Fry to golden brown, turning once
8. Remove from the fat with a slotted spoon and drain on a paper towel or cake rack
9. Leave plain or roll in powdered sugar

Makes about 3 dozen — this depends upon the size made. This is a good recipe. You can fry just half of the recipe and keep the rest covered and in the ice box until the next day.
