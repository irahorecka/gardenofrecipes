# Cole Slaw

## Ingredients

- 2 or more cups shredded cabbage
- 2 tbsp onion, sliced thin
- 2 tbsp diced tomatoes
- 1 tbsp diced green pepper
- 1/3 cup salad oil
- salt, to taste
- vinegar, to taste
- 3/4 to 1 tbsp sugar
- pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Mix together the shredded cabbage, onion, tomatoes, and green pepper
2. Mix together the salad oil, salt, vinegar, sugar, and pepper in a jar
3. Pour over the vegetable mixture
4. Keep covered in the ice box to marinate until ready to serve
