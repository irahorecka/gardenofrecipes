# Apple Dumplings

Delicious -- Don's favorite

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 3/4 cup shortening
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 medium apples
- 1 1/4 cups sugar
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 2 bottles 7-Up (7 oz. each)
- 1/4 cup butter or oleo
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- 6 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tbsp butter

## Instructions

1. Combine the 7-Up, 1 1/4 cups sugar, nutmeg, and 1/4 tsp cinnamon in a saucepan and dissolve
2. Add the 1/4 cup butter or oleo and melt
3. Mix the dry ingredients with the shortening and milk
4. Roll the dough and cut into 6 seven-inch squares
5. Pare and core the apples and place one whole apple on each square
6. Combine the 6 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon and use about 1 tbsp of this mixture per apple
7. Dot each apple with the 2 tbsp butter, then fold and pinch the corners of the dough around the apple
8. Arrange the dumplings in a 9x13 pan and pour the syrup over them
9. Bake at 425 degrees for 35 minutes
10. Serve warm with its own sauce
