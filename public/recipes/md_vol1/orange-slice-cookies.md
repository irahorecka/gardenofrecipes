# Orange Slice Cookies

From Dot

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups brown sugar
- 1/2 cup Crisco
- 1 tsp soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup cocoanut
- 1/2 cup oatmeal
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 pound orange slice candy
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. Cut up orange slices into small pieces. Put oil on the scissors as you cut, and it is easier to cut
2. Mix the orange slices with 1/2 cup of the above flour, and add cocoanut, chopped pecans, and oatmeal
3. Cream the Crisco and sugar
4. Beat in the eggs
5. Sift the dry ingredients and add to the creamed mixture
6. Add the rest of the ingredients and mix well
7. Roll into 1 inch balls
8. Put on a greased cookie sheet
9. Bake at 350 degrees for 12 minutes
