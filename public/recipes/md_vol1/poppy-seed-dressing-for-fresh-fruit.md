# Poppy Seed Dressing for Fresh Fruit

## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup sugar
- 1 tsp each dry mustard, paprika, and grated sweet onion
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup honey
- 1 cup salad oil
- 1 tbsp poppy seed
- 5 tbsp white vinegar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Mix together all ingredients
2. Use on fresh fruit
