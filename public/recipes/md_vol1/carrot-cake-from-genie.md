# Carrot Cake from Genie

_(Note: "Genie" is Ira's mom.)_

Very good

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp soda
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 1/4 cups Wesson oil
- 4 beaten eggs
- 3 cups grated carrots (1 one-pound bag)
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup coarsely chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. Sift together the flour, sugar, salt, soda, and cinnamon
2. Add the oil and beaten eggs. Beat well
3. Add the carrots, vanilla, and pecans
4. Stir well and bake in a greased and flour-dusted Bundt pan
5. Bake at 350 degrees about 35 to 45 minutes
6. You can make this in 2 large layer pans. Ice if wanted
