# Salad Dressing on Cabbage

Very good!

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Wesson oil
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp grated onion
- 1 tsp each salt, paprika, and celery seed or salt

## Instructions

1. Mix together all ingredients
2. Put this into a pint jar and shake vigorously
3. Keep in the ice box
