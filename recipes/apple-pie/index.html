Apple Pie

Very good

Ingredients

  • 4 or 5 nice sized apples, sliced (Joe: 6 Granny Smith)
  • 1/2 cup light brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup white sugar (Joe: 1/2 cup)
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • a bit of salt
  • 2 tsp cinnamon (Joe note)
  • 1 tsp nutmeg to suit your taste (Joe note)
  • lemon juice (for sprinkling)
  • 2 tbsp oleo (Joe: use butter)
  • milk, for brushing the top crust
  • sugar, for sprinkling on top

Instructions

  1. Mix together brown sugar, white sugar, flour, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg
  2. Mix well
  3. Put apples in unbaked pie shell
  4. Sprinkle with lemon juice
  5. Add the sugar mixture
  6. Put about 2 tbsp oleo over the apples
  7. Put on a top crust
  8. Make a few gashes in the crust
  9. Brush with milk and sprinkle sugar on top
  10. Bake at 350 degrees until done (Joe: ~50 minutes)

(Joe also notes: use a Crisco crust.)
