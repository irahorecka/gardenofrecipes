Oatmeal Cookies from School

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter or Crisco
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup chopped pecans
  • 3 cups oatmeal

Instructions

  1. Cream together the butter or Crisco, white sugar, brown sugar, vanilla, and eggs
  2. Add the flour, soda, salt, chopped pecans, and oatmeal, and add this to the creamed mixture
  3. May use as drop cookies, roll into balls, or wrap in waxed paper, chill, and slice
  4. Bake at 350 degrees
  5. May add spices if wanted
