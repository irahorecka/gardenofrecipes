Best Ever Waffles

May also be used as pancake batter.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs
  2. Add milk and oil
  3. Sift flour, salt, sugar, and baking powder
  4. Mix the milk mixture into the dry ingredients
  5. Bake in a greased waffle iron
