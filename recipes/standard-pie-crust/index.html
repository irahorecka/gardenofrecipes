Standard Pie Crust

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sifted flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup Crisco
  • 2 to 3 tbsp ice water

Instructions

  1. Blend together flour, salt, and Crisco
  2. Add 2 to 3 tablespoons ice water
  3. Roll out and put in a pie plate
  4. Pierce the crust
  5. Bake at 475 degrees
