# Kolache Recipe #1

Very good and tender — large recipe. No eggs

## Ingredients

- 1 yeast cake or 1 tbsp dry yeast
- 3 cups milk
- 6 level tbsp sugar
- 2/3 cup melted butter, oleo, Crisco, or Wesson oil
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- flour, for a soft dough

## Instructions

1. Scald the milk and cool so that it is warm only
2. Add the yeast and dissolve it in the milk
3. Use 1 tsp of the sugar and add it to the milk and yeast, because this makes it rise better
4. Add enough flour so that you have a batter like pancakes
5. Cover the dough with Saran wrap and let it rise until it is bubbly (you can skip this step of rising if you choose to do so)
6. Then add the salt, butter, and the rest of the sugar
7. Add enough flour to make a soft, buttery kind of dough. If you add too much flour, it will make tough kolaches
8. Again cover the dough with Saran wrap and let it rise
9. To test if the dough is ready, gently poke 2 fingers in the dough. If the indentation immediately comes back up, it needs to rise more. If the indentation remains, the dough is ready for the next step
10. Make small balls the size of small hen eggs. You may dip the dough out by tablespoons, or butter your hands and pinch off small balls of dough
11. Put in greased pans, about 2 inches apart, on a large cookie sheet. You can put 12 to 15 on a pan
12. Butter the sides and top. Cover and let rise in the oven (put on no fire unless it is the pilot light)
13. Make an indentation with 2 fingers, one from each hand, in the center of each ball, and stretch the dough gently apart for the filling
14. Let these balls rise about 15 to 20 minutes
15. Put the fruit filling in the center of each. You may need to stretch that little opening one more time
16. Put streusel topping on top of the filling
17. Put in a preheated 400 degree oven and bake about 15 minutes. This depends upon your oven
18. Butter the top of each when you take them from the oven
19. Sprinkle sugar on top of each
