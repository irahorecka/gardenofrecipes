# Fruit Cocktail Cake

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 2 tsp soda
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 no. 303 can fruit cocktail (this is 2 cups with juice)

## Instructions

1. Mix the eggs and sugar
2. Add and mix the rest of the ingredients
3. Mix and bake at 325 degrees until done
