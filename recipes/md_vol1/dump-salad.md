# Dump Salad

From Maria

## Ingredients

- 1 carton cottage cheese
- 1 box orange Jello
- 1 can crushed pineapple, drained
- 1 small can mandarin oranges, drained
- 1 small carton Cool Whip

## Instructions

1. Mix the cottage cheese with the orange Jello
2. Add the drained pineapple and mandarin oranges to the cheese mixture
3. Stir in the Cool Whip
4. Cool covered in the ice box for one hour, if you can wait that long
