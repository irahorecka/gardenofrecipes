# Penny Hot Rolls

From Gay Bippert — very good and tender

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp dry yeast or 1 cake of yeast
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp salad oil
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup hot water
- 1 egg
- 3 to 3 1/2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in the 1/2 cup warm water
2. In a large bowl, put in the hot water, sugar, salt, and oil. Cool to lukewarm
3. Stir in the beaten egg, yeast, and 2 cups flour
4. Beat for 2 minutes with an electric mixer at medium
5. Stir in the rest of the flour to make a soft dough
6. Beat 3 minutes with a spoon (who counts it)
7. Stir down, cover tightly, and let rise again
8. When doubled, make into rolls. Let rise
9. Bake at 375 to 400 degrees for 12 to 13 minutes. This depends on your own oven
10. These rolls can also be put in the refrigerator after they are mixed. The following day, take them out and let them come to room temperature, then follow the same procedure as listed above for baking
