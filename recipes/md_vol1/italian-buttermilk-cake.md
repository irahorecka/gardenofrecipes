# Italian Buttermilk Cake

## Ingredients

- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp soda
- 5 eggs, separated
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 stick oleo
- 1/2 cup Crisco
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup chopped pecans
- 1 small can coconut

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees
2. Combine the soda and buttermilk
3. Cream the sugar, Crisco, and oleo
4. Add the egg yolks
5. Add the flour alternately with the buttermilk mixture
6. Add the vanilla, pecans, and coconut
7. Beat the egg whites until stiff and fold into the batter
8. Bake for 25 minutes in 2 greased 9-inch pans

## Icing

### Ingredients

- 1 8-ounce package cream cheese
- 1 stick oleo
- 1 box powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla

### Instructions

1. Beat the cream cheese, oleo, powdered sugar, and vanilla together
2. Spread over the cooled cake
