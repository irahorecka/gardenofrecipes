# Pie Crust Dough

Helen Najvar Pie Crust Dough (my cousin)

## Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups Crisco
- 1 cup cold water

## Instructions

1. Mix the dry ingredients well
2. Add the water and mix
3. Roll into balls
4. Cover and chill 2 to 4 hours or overnight, then it is easier to handle
