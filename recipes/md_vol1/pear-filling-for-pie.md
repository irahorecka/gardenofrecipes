# Pear Filling for Pie

## Ingredients

- about 3 cups fresh sliced pears or canned pears, drained well
- 1/2 cup sugar, for posipka
- 1/2 cup flour, for posipka
- about 1/2 to 1/4 cup melted oleo, for posipka
- cinnamon and cocoanut, if wanted
- oleo, for topping the pears
- sugar and cinnamon, for the top crust

## Instructions

1. Line the pie pan with crust
2. Make the posipka by mixing together the sugar, flour, and melted oleo. Crumble this together. You may add cinnamon and cocoanut if wanted
3. Cover the crust with posipka
4. Place the pears on the posipka and put some oleo on top
5. Add more posipka on top
6. Cover with the top crust and crimp the edges
7. With a fork, punch holes in the top crust
8. Butter the top and sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon
9. Bake at 350 to 375 degrees for 45 minutes

I am sure that apples could also be used.
