# All Seasoning Salt

## Ingredients

- 1 cup salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp white pepper
- 1/4 tsp celery salt
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp garlic salt

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients and mix well
2. Keep in a big shaker

This is good on pork chops and liver. I am sure it is also good on chicken.
