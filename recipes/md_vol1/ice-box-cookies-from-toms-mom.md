# Ice Box Cookies from Tom's Mom

## Ingredients

- 1 cup oleo
- 2 eggs
- 4 cups flour
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 1 tsp soda
- 1 cup chopped pecans
- 1 tsp cream of tartar

## Instructions

1. Mix and make into a sausage roll
2. Wrap in waxed paper or foil and chill
3. Slice thin and put on pan
4. Bake at 350 degrees
