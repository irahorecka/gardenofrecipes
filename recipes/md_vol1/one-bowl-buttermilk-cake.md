# One Bowl Buttermilk Cake

Very good

## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups sifted flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup cocoa
- 1/2 cup soft shortening
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 1/4 cups sugar
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 beaten eggs

## Instructions

1. Sift the dry ingredients into a bowl
2. Add the shortening and 2/3 cup buttermilk
3. Beat 2 minutes at moderate speed
4. Add the rest of the buttermilk and the beaten eggs
5. Beat for 2 minutes
6. Add the vanilla
7. Pour into 2 greased and floured 8-inch pans
8. Bake at 350 degrees until it tests done
