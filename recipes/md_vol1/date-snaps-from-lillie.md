# Date Snaps from Lillie

## Ingredients

- 3 cups sugar
- 1 cup oleo
- 1 pound pecans
- 1/2 tsp cloves
- 1/4 cup water
- 3 eggs
- 1 pound dates
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp soda
- 3 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Cream the sugar and oleo
2. Add the eggs
3. Sift the dry ingredients
4. Add to the creamed mixture with the water
5. Grind or chop finely the dates and pecans
6. Mix with the cookie dough
7. Make into a roll and put in waxed paper
8. Chill, slice, and bake
