# Cocoanut Pecan Cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 3/4 cup shortening
- 1 1/4 cups brown sugar
- 2 cups oatmeal
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup cocoanut
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. Mix and bake (I think you make small balls and then put on the cookie sheet)
2. Bake at 350 degrees

The recipe states it makes 70 medium cookies.
