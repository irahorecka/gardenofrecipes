# White Fruit Cookies

From Granny Schott

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp soda
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup raisins (white, if possible)
- 1 cup pecans
- 1 cup dates
- 1 cup cocoanut
- flour, enough to roll out
- sugar, for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Cream the butter with the sugar
2. Add the eggs
3. Mix together the soda and buttermilk
4. Add the nutmeg, cinnamon, and salt
5. Mix this with the butter and sugar mixture
6. Add the raisins, pecans, dates, and cocoanut
7. Add enough flour to roll out
8. Sprinkle with sugar
9. Cut and bake

Granny made these years ago at Haby's Bakery.
