# Lola's Pickles

Delicious

## Ingredients

- 3 inch size cucumbers, clean and dry, enough to fill quart jars
- dill
- some onions
- garlic
- 2 to 3 chili petines, depending on your taste
- 1 grape leaf, if you want, per jar
- 1 tbsp canning salt, per jar
- 3 tbsp sugar, per jar
- 1/3 cup 90% vinegar, per jar
- water, to fill (rain water is best)

## Instructions

1. Fill quart jars with cucumbers
2. Put dill, onions, garlic, and chili petines in the jar. Also put in a grape leaf if you want. Put all of this in the jar first
3. Then stuff cucumbers in jars
4. For each jar, add the canning salt, sugar, and vinegar
5. Fill the jar with water until you get to the neck
6. Seal the jars and put them in a water bath to boil until the cucumbers turn color
7. Take them out and let cool
8. Remove the screw bands the next day

I use National brand vinegar, which has a picture of a wooden barrel on the label — this is the best canning vinegar. Rain water is best, so get ready ahead of canning time; put dish pans out for rain water and strain into milk jugs, save this for canning (a good hint).

An old school friend of mine uses the same recipe and the same procedure but only uses 1/2 cup sugar instead of 1/3 cup.
