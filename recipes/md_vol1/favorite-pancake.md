# Favorite Pancake

From Better Homes and Gardens Small Book.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cup sifted flour
- 3 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt _(Note: adjusted to 1/2 tsp)_
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 beaten egg
- 2 tbsp salad oil
- 1 cup milk

## Instructions

1. Sift dry ingredients and put into a wide bowl
2. Add the milk, egg, and oil
3. Mix together
4. Stir only until dampened
5. Bake on a hot griddle
6. Makes 12 dollar size or 8 4-inch size pancakes
7. For thinner pancakes, add 2 tbsp more milk to the batter
