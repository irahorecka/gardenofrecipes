# Dressing for Texas Slaw

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup vinegar
- 1/3 cup oil
- a bit of water
- 1 tsp celery seed
- 1/2 tsp mustard seed
- 1/2 tsp caraway seed

## Instructions

1. Mix together the sugar, vinegar, oil, water, celery seed, mustard seed, and caraway seed
2. Boil the above
3. Strain this over the cabbage while the mixture is hot
