# Basic Pancake

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup milk _(Note: or 1 cup buttermilk, or a mix of milk and buttermilk)_
- 1 cup sifted flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 2 tbsp cooking oil, or melted butter or oleo

## Instructions

1. Sift flour, salt, sugar, and baking powder in a wide bowl
2. Combine egg, milk, and oil
3. Slowly stir this liquid into the dry ingredients
4. Mix only until dampened. Batter should be lumpy
5. Use 3 tbsp for each pancake. Allow space between them
6. Allow them to spread to 4 inches for a thinner pancake
7. If batter stands a while, you may add an additional 1/4 tsp baking powder
8. Add 2 to 3 tbsp more milk for a thinner pancake

## Variations

- For blueberry pancakes, add 1 tbsp blueberries on top of the pancake after it's in the pan. Cook until golden on both sides
- For pineapple, add 1/2 cup drained pineapple to the batter
- For banana, put 4 slices of banana on the skillet and then pour batter on top
