# Dumplings

Very good

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup flour _(Note: corrected in handwriting to 1 cup flour)_
- 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 1/3 cup milk

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, salt, egg, and milk
2. Drop by teaspoons into the hot gravy
3. Cover and simmer covered for 15 minutes
4. Add chicken (or you may serve the chicken extra on a platter)

I double this dumpling recipe.

_(Note: handwritten addition — try adding 1-2 tbsp parmesan cheese and 1 tbsp minced parsley.)_
