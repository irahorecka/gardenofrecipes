# Snickerdoodles Cookies

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Crisco (butter or oleo)
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp cream of tartar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 2 3/4 cups sifted flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 tbsp sugar and 2 tsp cinnamon, for rolling

## Instructions

1. Mix this and chill in the ice box for about 1 to 2 hours
2. Roll into small balls
3. Roll each ball in a mixture of 2 tablespoons sugar and 2 teaspoons cinnamon
4. Put on cookie sheet about 2 inches apart
5. Bake at 375 degrees for 8 to 10 minutes
