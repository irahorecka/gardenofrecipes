# Kolache Recipe #2

From Carol Kitchen in San Antonio

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp dry yeast
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 2 cups scalded milk
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup shortening or Crisco, plus 2 tbsp
- 2 beaten eggs
- 7 or 7 1/2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Mix the dry yeast, 2 tsp sugar, and warm water together. Let the yeast dissolve in this mixture
2. Add the 1/2 cup sugar, salt, and shortening to the scalded milk. Let cool
3. Add the eggs and the yeast mixture to the milk mixture
4. Gradually stir in the flour. Mix by hand, but add the flour slowly — you only need enough to make a soft dough
5. Butter the top of the dough. Cover with foil or Saran wrap and let rise
6. Make into kolaches as directed in Kolache Recipe #1
7. Bake at 400 degrees for 15 minutes or until done. Butter the top of each
