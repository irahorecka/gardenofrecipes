# Macaroni Salad

From Gail from Wisconsin

## Ingredients

- 1 stick (1/4 pound) oleo
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp flour
- 1 tsp dry mustard
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup cream
- 1/2 cup vinegar
- 3 1/2 cups cooked elbow macaroni
- 1/4 cup each chopped onions, green pepper, and celery

## Instructions

1. Melt the oleo
2. Mix together the sugar, flour, and dry mustard. Stir this into the melted oleo
3. Beat the eggs and add to the mixture
4. Add the water, cream, and vinegar. Mix well
5. Cook over low heat until it boils and thickens
6. Cool this and pour over the macaroni, onions, green pepper, and celery

Note: this is a good salad to take to a family gathering because it doesn't have any mayonnaise in it to spoil. Also, this recipe makes a lot of dressing. I don't use it all at first. I put just as much as needed the first day. Then I add more the next day.
