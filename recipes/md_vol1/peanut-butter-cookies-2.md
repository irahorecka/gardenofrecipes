# Peanut Butter Cookies #2

From Mrs. Terry Reus

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup shortening
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1 1/4 cups sifted flour
- 3/4 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Cream together the shortening, white sugar, egg, peanut butter, and brown sugar
2. Add the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt
3. Mix and chill the dough
4. Roll into small balls
5. Flatten with a fork and make criss cross designs with it
6. Bake at 375 degrees for 10 to 12 minutes
