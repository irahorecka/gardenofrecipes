# Basic Coffee Cake or Doughnuts

## Ingredients

- 1 yeast cake or 1 tbsp dry yeast
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 stick oleo
- 1 1/2 cups milk, scalded and cooled
- 3 beaten eggs
- 4 to 5 cups flour, enough for a soft dough

## Instructions

1. Mix together the yeast, sugar, oleo, milk, eggs, and flour
2. Beat until elastic
3. Cover. Let rise until double in size and use as wanted
4. May also use for rolls
