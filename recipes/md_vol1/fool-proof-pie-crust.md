# Fool Proof Pie Crust

From my friend Gail Grunenberger of Wisconsin

Makes 5 single 9-inch pie crusts

## Ingredients

- 4 cups flour
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp vinegar or orange juice
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 3/4 cups vegetable shortening
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 egg

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, shortening, salt, and sugar with a pastry blender
2. In a separate bowl, beat the remaining ingredients
3. Combine both mixtures, stirring until all ingredients are moistened
4. Roll into 5 balls
5. Wrap in plastic wrap and chill at least 15 minutes or more

Can be stored in the refrigerator wrapped for 3 days or in the freezer for 4 to 6 months.
