# Plain Cake from School

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup oleo
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 3 tsp baking powder
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Cream the butter and sugar
2. Add the eggs
3. Sift the dry ingredients together
4. Add the dry ingredients alternately with the milk
5. Add the vanilla
6. Bake at 350 degrees
