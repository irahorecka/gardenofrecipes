# Oatmeal Cake

## Ingredients

- 1 1/3 cups boiling water
- 1 cup oatmeal
- 1/2 cup butter or oleo
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp soda
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Pour the boiling water over the oatmeal and set aside
2. Cream the oleo and sugars, then beat in the eggs and vanilla
3. Add the sifted dry ingredients
4. Then add the oatmeal mixture
5. Mix and put into greased and flour-dusted pans
6. Bake at 350 degrees

## Topping

### Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup cream
- 1/2 cup oleo
- 1 cup pecans and coconut

### Instructions

1. Boil and spread on the cake
