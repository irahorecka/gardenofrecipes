# Old Fashioned Cole Slaw Dressing

## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup evaporated milk
- 2 to 4 tbsp sugar
- 2 to 3 tbsp vinegar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- black pepper, to taste
- 3 cups shredded cabbage

## Instructions

1. Combine the evaporated milk, sugar, vinegar, salt, and black pepper
2. Pour over the shredded cabbage
3. Mix and chill for 1 hour or more
