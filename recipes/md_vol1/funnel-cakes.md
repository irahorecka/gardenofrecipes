# Funnel Cakes

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 cups milk
- 4 cups flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- fat, for frying

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs and milk together
2. Sift the flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder together, then add to the egg mixture and beat until smooth
3. Hold a finger over the bottom of a funnel and pour about 1/2 cup of batter into the funnel
4. Hold the funnel over the hot fat and release your finger, moving the funnel in a circular motion to form a spiral cake
5. Fry at 375 degrees until golden brown, then turn and brown the other side
6. Drain and sprinkle with sugar

Makes about 3 dozen. This is a good recipe. You can fry just half of the recipe and keep the rest covered and in the ice box until the next day.
