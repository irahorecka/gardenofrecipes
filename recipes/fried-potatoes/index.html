Fried Potatoes

Ingredients

  • potatoes, cut into very small pieces
  • onions, diced
  • green peppers, diced (if wanted)
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into very small pieces
  2. Dice onions (and green peppers, if wanted)
  3. Add salt and pepper
  4. Fry until done
